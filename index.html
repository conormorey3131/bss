Corporate tax in the UAE is just 9%. All UAE companies need to register with the FTA and submit annual tax returns.

VAT

VAT in the UAE is one of the world's lowest - only 5%. Businesses with an annual revenue exceeding 375,000 AED are required to register for VAT with the FTA.
