Company Registration in Dubai – Your Step-by-Step Guide

Starting a business in Dubai is an exciting venture, but it involves a series of essential steps and compliance with local regulations. At Business Setup Solutions, we simplify the process and guide you through every stage of company registration. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you get started:

Choose the Right Business Activity and Legal Structure

Begin by determining the type of business activity you want to engage in and selecting the appropriate legal structure. We'll help you make the right choices to align with your business goals.

Select a Unique Company Name

Selecting a unique company name is crucial. We'll assist you in choosing a name that complies with Dubai's naming conventions and guidelines.

Prepare the Required Documents

Our experts will guide you in gathering the necessary documents, which may include passports and visa copies of shareholders and directors, a non-objection certificate (NOC) if applicable, and other specific documents depending on your chosen business structure and activity.

Determine Share Capital

Depending on your business structure, you may need to specify the minimum share capital required for your company. We'll ensure you understand the capital requirements.

Obtain Initial Approval

Our team will assist you in applying for initial approval from the relevant government department, such as the Discover Department of Economy and Tourism in Dubai (DED) or the respective Free Zone authority, depending on your chosen business location.

Legalize and Notarize Documents

Certain documents may require notarization and attestation, and we'll help you with this process.

Rent a Physical Office Space

If your business structure requires a physical office, we'll help you find a suitable space that meets the Dubai authorities' requirements.

Submit Documents and Fees

We'll manage the submission of your application, along with the required documents and fees, to the relevant authorities. This may vary based on your chosen business structure and location.

Wait for Approval

While your application is under review, we'll ensure all necessary steps are taken, and you can focus on preparing for your business's exciting launch.

Receive the License

Once your application is approved, you'll receive your business license, granting you the legal right to conduct business in Dubai.

Comply with Ongoing Requirements

Our ongoing support includes helping you renew your business license annually and ensuring you meet all regulatory and financial compliance requirements.

Navigating the process of company registration in Dubai may seem complex, but with the guidance of Business Setup Solutions, it becomes a streamlined and efficient experience. We're your trusted partner in business success, offering expert assistance every step of the way. Start your Dubai business journey with confidence and contact us today for a consultation.
