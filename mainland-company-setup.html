Mainland Company Setup

For businesses looking to operate within the UAE's local market, a mainland company is the ideal choice. Our Mainland Company Setup services provide you with a seamless and efficient way to establish your presence in the heart of the UAE's economy. We will guide you through the entire process, from securing the necessary approvals from the Department of Economic Development (DED) to helping you find a reliable local sponsor.

Key Benefits

  • Freedom to trade with any company in the UAE.
  • Ability to bid on government contracts.
  • No restrictions on the number of visas you can apply for.
  • Flexibility to operate from any location in the UAE.

Our Process

Our team will ensure that your business is fully compliant with all local laws and regulations, giving you the freedom to trade with any company in the UAE and bid on government contracts. With our expert support, you can unlock the full potential of the UAE market.
