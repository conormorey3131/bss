Golden Visa Program

The Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa that allows foreign talents to live, work, or study in the UAE while enjoying exclusive benefits. It is designed for investors, entrepreneurs, scientists, outstanding students, and humanitarian pioneers who have made significant contributions to their field.

Key Benefits of the Golden Visa

  • 10-year renewable residence visa.
  • No need for a national sponsor.
  • Ability to sponsor family members, including spouses and children regardless of their ages.
  • Sponsorship of an unlimited number of domestic helpers.
  • Extended visa validity for family members after the death of the primary holder.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for the Golden Visa is determined by the applicant's profession, investment, or talent. The main categories include:

  • Investors: Public investments of at least AED 2 million.
  • Entrepreneurs: Founder of a project with a capital of at least AED 500,000.
  • Exceptional Talents: Doctors, scientists, artists, and inventors.
  • Outstanding Students: High-performing students in UAE secondary schools and universities.
