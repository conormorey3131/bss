Free Zone Setup

The UAE is home to over 40 free zones, each offering a unique set of benefits and incentives for businesses. Our Free Zone Setup services are designed to help you navigate this landscape and choose the free zone that is best suited to your business needs. We provide expert advice on the various free zones, taking into account factors such as your industry, budget, and long-term goals.

Key Benefits

  • 100% foreign ownership of your business.
  • 0% corporate and personal income tax.
  • 100% repatriation of profits and capital.
  • Access to a world-class business environment and infrastructure.

Our Process

Once you have made your decision, we will handle the entire setup process, from preparing the necessary documents to liaising with the free zone authorities. With our assistance, you can take full advantage of the benefits of a free zone company, including 100% foreign ownership, 0% corporate and personal income tax, and 100% repatriation of profits and capital.
