Real Estate Investor Visa

The Real Estate Investor Visa offers a renewable residence visa to individuals who invest in the UAE's property market. This visa is an excellent option for those looking to establish a long-term presence in the country through real estate ownership.

Key Benefits of the Real Estate Investor Visa

  • Renewable residence visa for 2 years.
  • Ability to sponsor your spouse and children.
  • No requirement to establish a company in the UAE.
  • Enjoy the benefits of being a UAE resident, including opening bank accounts.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the Real Estate Investor Visa, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Property Value: Own a property with a gross value of at least AED 750,000.
  • Title Deed: The property must be fully paid for and a title deed must be issued in the applicant's name.
  • Freehold Property: The property must be located in a freehold area designated for foreign ownership.
