IFZA (International Free Zone Authority)

Introduction to IFZA

IFZA is one of the most affordable and fastest-growing free zones in the UAE. It offers a wide range of business activities and license types, making it an ideal choice for startups and SMEs.

Located in Dubai, IFZA provides a highly competitive and business-friendly environment for entrepreneurs from around the world.

Benefits of Setting Up in IFZA

  • Cost-effective business setup packages
  • Fast and easy company formation
  • No physical office requirement
  • Wide range of business activities
  • 100% foreign ownership

Business Activities in IFZA

IFZA offers a variety of license options, including professional, commercial, and industrial licenses. This flexibility allows you to conduct a wide range of business activities under one license.

Company Formation Process

The company formation process in IFZA is designed to be quick and efficient. With our assistance, you can have your company registered and licensed in just a few days.
