# Website Summary: Business Setup Solutions Dubai

## Overview

This website belongs to "Business Setup Solutions Dubai," a company that provides comprehensive services for setting up and managing businesses in the UAE. The site is a single-page layout designed to be informative and user-friendly, offering a clear path for potential clients to understand the services and get in touch. It is available in both English and Russian.

## Website Structure and Content

### 1. Header and Navigation
- **Logo:** Features the company logo.
- **Company Name:** "Business Setup Solutions Dubai".
- **Language Selector:** Buttons to switch between English and Russian.
- **Navigation:** Links to different sections of the page: Home, Services, About Us, and Contact.

### 2. Hero Section
- **Main Headline:** "Business Setup in Dubai, Company Formation in UAE".
- **Offer:** "Starting from 5,500 AED | Registration 1 day | Guaranteed bank account".
- **Call to Action:** A button to "Get a Free Consultation".

### 3. Video Presentation
- A section with an embedded video presentation about the company's services. The video source changes based on the selected language.

### 4. Services
A list of the core services offered:
- Company Formation
- Free Zone Setup
- Mainland Company Setup
- Offshore Company Setup
- Visa Services
- Bank Account Opening
- Residence Visa
- Accounting Support Solutions
- Relocation Services

### 5. Contact Section
- **Consultation Form:** A form for users to submit their name, email, phone number, and a message.
- **Contact Numbers:** A list of phone numbers for their branches in Ireland, Kyrgyzstan, Russia, and Dubai.

### 6. UAE Business Setup Process
- This section outlines the five key steps to establishing a company in the UAE, focusing on the audit process.

### 7. Informational Sections
The website provides detailed information on various aspects of setting up a business in the UAE:
- **Free Zones:** Highlights popular free zones like DMCC, IFZA, DIFC, JAFZA, DAFZA, and RAKEZ.
- **UAE Taxes:** Explains the 9% corporate tax, 5% VAT, and the absence of personal income tax.
- **Dubai Trade Licenses:** Details different types of licenses, such as E-commerce and Car Lease licenses.
- **UAE Residency Visas:** Information on Golden, Real Estate Investor, and Partner visas.
- **Bank Accounts:** Describes options for Corporate and Resident bank accounts.

### 8. Footer
- **Copyright:** "© 2025 Business Setup Solutions Dubai".
- **Credit:** "Powered by Morey Digital".

## Technical Overview

The website is a static, single-page site. It uses JavaScript to provide multilingual support. The text content for English and Russian is stored in separate `.js` files (`en.js` and `ru.js`) and is dynamically loaded into the HTML elements based on the user's language selection. The site also includes a hamburger menu for mobile navigation and subtle scroll animations for a better user experience.
