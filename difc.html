DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre)

Introduction to DIFC

DIFC is the leading financial hub for the Middle East, Africa and South Asia (MEASA) region. It has its own independent, internationally regulated regulator and judicial system, common law framework, global financial exchange, tax-friendly regime, and a large business community.

Benefits of Setting Up in DIFC

  • Independent legal and regulatory framework
  • 100% foreign ownership
  • 0% tax on income and profits for 50 years
  • Access to a deep pool of skilled professionals
  • A vibrant ecosystem of financial and non-financial companies

Business Activities in DIFC

DIFC is the ideal base for financial and professional services firms. It offers licenses for banking, insurance, wealth management, and capital markets, as well as non-financial activities such as consulting and legal services.

Company Formation Process

The company formation process in DIFC is rigorous to ensure the highest standards of quality and compliance. Our team has the expertise to navigate the process and help you establish your presence in this prestigious financial centre.
