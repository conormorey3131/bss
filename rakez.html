RAKEZ (Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone)

Introduction to RAKEZ

RAKEZ is a powerhouse business and industrial hub in the UAE that offers customised solutions to free zone and non-free zone businesses. From freelancers and startups to SMEs and industrialists, RAKEZ has a solution for every type of business.

Benefits of Setting Up in RAKEZ

  • Cost-effective business setup packages
  • Wide range of industrial and commercial facilities
  • Easy access to major logistical hubs
  • A one-stop shop for all government services
  • 100% foreign ownership

Business Activities in RAKEZ

RAKEZ caters to a diverse range of industries, including manufacturing, trading, services, and education. It offers over 100 business activities and a variety of license types to suit your needs.

Company Formation Process

The company formation process in RAKEZ is simple and straightforward. Our team will assist you with the entire process, from choosing the right license to obtaining your visa.
