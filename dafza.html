DAFZA (Dubai Airport Free Zone)

Introduction to DAFZA

DAFZA is one of the most prestigious and advanced free zones in the world. Located adjacent to Dubai International Airport, it offers a gateway to the Middle East and unparalleled access to global markets.

Benefits of Setting Up in DAFZA

  • Direct access to Dubai International Airport
  • State-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities
  • 24/7 customs services and rapid cargo clearance
  • A dedicated government service center
  • 100% foreign ownership

Business Activities in DAFZA

DAFZA is ideal for businesses in sectors such as aviation, logistics, pharmaceuticals, IT, and luxury goods. It offers a range of licenses, including trade, service, and industrial licenses.

Company Formation Process

The company formation process in DAFZA is streamlined and efficient. Our team will assist you with every step, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free setup experience.
