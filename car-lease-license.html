Car Lease License

The automotive and tourism sectors in the UAE are thriving, making a car lease or rental business a lucrative venture for Irish investors. A Car Lease license is required to operate this type of business, and we can guide you through the specific requirements and regulations set by the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA).

Key Benefits

  • Tap into Dubai's booming tourism and automotive markets.
  • High demand for both short-term rentals and long-term leasing.
  • Clear regulatory framework under the RTA.
  • Full support with fleet registration and insurance.

Our Process

We provide comprehensive support for obtaining your Car Lease license. This includes securing initial approval from the RTA, preparing all necessary documentation, and ensuring your business is fully compliant with all transport regulations. Let us handle the paperwork so you can focus on building your fleet.
