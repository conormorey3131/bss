JAFZA (Jebel Ali Free Zone)

Introduction to JAFZA

JAFZA is one of the world's largest free zones and a leading hub for trade and logistics. Located next to the Jebel Ali Port, the world's ninth busiest port, and Al Maktoum International Airport, JAFZA offers unparalleled connectivity to global markets.

Benefits of Setting Up in JAFZA

  • Unrivalled access to global markets
  • 100% foreign ownership
  • 0% import or re-export duties
  • No currency restrictions
  • On-site customs facilities

Business Activities in JAFZA

JAFZA is ideal for companies in the logistics, manufacturing, and trading sectors. It offers a wide range of licenses and facilities, including warehouses, offices, and industrial land.

Company Formation Process

Setting up a company in JAFZA involves a comprehensive process to ensure compliance with its high standards. Our team will manage the entire process for you, from document submission to license issuance.
