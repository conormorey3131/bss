E-Commerce License

The UAE's e-commerce market is booming, offering a massive opportunity for Irish entrepreneurs. An E-Commerce license allows you to sell goods and services online, tapping into a dynamic and growing digital economy. We provide end-to-end support to help you launch your online business in Dubai quickly and efficiently.

Key Benefits

  • Access to the fast-growing UAE and GCC online markets.
  • 100% foreign ownership in free zones.
  • Low setup costs and operational overheads.
  • Full compliance with UAE e-commerce regulations.

Our Process

We handle everything from choosing the right jurisdiction (free zone or mainland) to registering your trade name and obtaining the necessary approvals. Our team ensures your e-commerce business is set up for success, allowing you to focus on building your online brand.
