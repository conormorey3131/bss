DMCC (Dubai Multi Commodities Centre)

Introduction to DMCC

DMCC is a government entity established in 2002 to enhance commodity trade flows through Dubai. It is the world's most interconnected Free Zone, and the leading trade and enterprise hub for commodities.

DMCC is home to a vibrant community, innovative infrastructure, world-class services and a stunning choice of properties, all of which are designed to help your business thrive.

Benefits of Setting Up in DMCC

  • 100% foreign business ownership
  • 0% corporate and personal income tax
  • Strategic location in the heart of new Dubai
  • A full range of business activities
  • World-class infrastructure

Business Activities in DMCC

DMCC offers a wide range of business activities, including trading, services, and industrial activities. Whether you are a startup or a multinational corporation, DMCC has a solution for you.

Company Formation Process

The company formation process in DMCC is straightforward and can be completed in a few weeks. Our team will guide you through every step, from initial application to receiving your business license.
