Partner Visa

A Partner Visa is available to foreign nationals who own shares in an existing mainland or free zone company in the UAE. This visa allows you to live and work in the country, making it an ideal choice for business owners and entrepreneurs looking to elevate their presence in the region.

Key Benefits of the Partner Visa

  • Residency in the UAE for the duration of your partnership.
  • Ability to sponsor your family members.
  • Full access to UAE banking and healthcare services.
  • Freedom to travel in and out of the country.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a Partner Visa, you must meet the following conditions:

  • Company Shares: Own shares in a UAE-based company with a minimum value of AED 72,000.
  • Medical Fitness: Pass a medical fitness test at a government-approved health center.
  • Security Clearance: Obtain security clearance from the relevant authorities.
